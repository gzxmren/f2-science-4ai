🔌 家庭用电

家庭用电是 AC（交流电），与电池的 DC（直流电）不同。

Fuse（保险丝）： 电流过大时熔断，切断电路 → 保护安全。

⚠️ 用电安全 常考

⚡
Short circuit
短路 → 电流极大 → 火灾
📦
Overloading
过载 → 太多电器同时用
🌍
Earthing
接地 → 漏电时导入大地

💰 电费计算 必考计算

Energy (kWh) = Power (kW) × Time (h)
电能 = 功率 × 时间
Cost = Energy (kWh) × Price per kWh
电费 = 电能 × 每度电价格
📌 示例： A 1000 W heater is used for 3 hours. The cost of electricity is $1.2 per kWh.

Energy = 1 kW × 3 h = 3 kWh
Cost = 3 × $1.2 = $3.6
📌 示例： A 2 kW air conditioner is used for 5 hours. Cost = $1.5 per kWh.

Energy = 2 × 5 = 10 kWh
Cost = 10 × $1.5 = $15
⚠️ 考试关键： 先换单位！W → kW（除以 1000），min → h（除以 60）。公式写出来就有步骤分。

✏️ 英文题目

Q1: What is the function of a fuse in household electricity?
It melts and breaks the circuit when the current is too large, preventing damage or fire.
Q2: What is overloading?
Overloading happens when too many electrical appliances are connected to the same circuit, drawing too much current.
Q3: A 1500 W microwave is used for 2 hours. Electricity costs $1.2 per kWh. Find the cost.
Power = 1500 W = 1.5 kW. Energy = 1.5 × 2 = 3 kWh. Cost = 3 × $1.2 = $3.6
Q4: What is the difference between AC and DC?
AC (alternating current) changes direction; DC (direct current) flows in one direction. Household electricity uses AC.
