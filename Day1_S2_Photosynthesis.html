🌞 光合作用是什么？

"Photo" = 光, "synthesis" = 合成。光合作用是植物利用 light energy（光能） 制造食物的过程。

文字方程式 必考

CO₂ + H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ (glucose) + O₂
需要 light（光）+ chlorophyll（叶绿素）

能量转换

Light energy（光能） → Chemical energy in food（化学能）

发生场所

植物含有 chlorophyll（叶绿素） 的细胞，主要在 leaves（叶子）。

📈 影响光合作用的因素

  1. Light intensity（光照强度）— 越强越快
  2. CO₂ concentration（二氧化碳浓度）— 越高越快
  3. Temperature（温度）— 适温最快
  4. Colour of light（光颜色）— 红光和蓝光效率最高

🔬 关键实验 常考

Experiment 7.3 — Testing for starch（淀粉检测）

用 iodine solution（碘液） 检测叶子中是否有淀粉。

有淀粉 → iodine 变成 blue-black（蓝黑色）

没有淀粉 → iodine 保持 brown（棕色）

Destarching（耗尽淀粉）

实验前把植物放在 dark place（黑暗处） 1–2 天，

让植物用完已有的淀粉，确保实验检测到的是新产生的淀粉。

Experiment 7.4 — Oxygen from photosynthesis

光合作用释放 oxygen（氧气）。

用 glowing splint 测试 → relights（复燃）

Experiment 7.5 — 不同颜色光的影响

不同颜色的光对光合作用速率有不同影响。

✏️ 英文题目（点击看答案）

Q1: What is the word equation for photosynthesis?
CO₂ + H₂O → glucose + O₂ (need light, chlorophyll)
Q2: What test is used to show that photosynthesis has produced starch?
Iodine solution test. If starch is present, iodine turns blue-black.
Q3: What is the energy conversion in photosynthesis?
Light energy → chemical energy (in food)
Q4: Name ONE factor that affects the rate of photosynthesis.
Light intensity / CO₂ concentration / Temperature / Colour of light
Q5: Why do we put a plant in a dark place before testing for starch?
To destarch the plant, so that any starch found is produced during the experiment.
Q6: Where does photosynthesis take place in a plant?
In the leaves, in cells that contain chlorophyll.

📘 Assignment Book 真题 — 7.2 Photosynthesis

以下题目来自 Assignment Book 2A。

📝 True or False: Plants release chemical energy by photosynthesis.
False. Plants convert light energy to chemical energy (they don't release it).
📝 True or False: Iodine turns blue-black if starch is present.
False. Iodine turns from brown to blue-black if starch is present.
📝 MC: What is the major product of photosynthesis?
Starch (glucose is stored as starch)
📝 MC: Through photosynthesis, plants convert ___ energy to ___ energy.
Light energy → chemical energy
📝 MC: Which are needed for photosynthesis? (1) Light (2) Water (3) Oxygen (4) Chlorophyll (5) CO₂
(1), (2), (4) and (5) only — oxygen is NOT needed
