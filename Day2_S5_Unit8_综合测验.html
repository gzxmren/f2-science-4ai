✏️ 综合测验（共 8 题）

Q1: What is a conductor? Give ONE example.
导体的定义和例子
A material that allows electricity to flow through it. E.g. copper, aluminum, graphite.
Q2: How is an ammeter connected in a circuit? How is a voltmeter connected?
安培计和伏特计的接法
Ammeter: in series. Voltmeter: in parallel.
Q3: Compare a series circuit and a parallel circuit. (Give TWO differences)
串联和并联的两个区别
(1) In series, current is the same everywhere; in parallel, current splits in branches. (2) In series, removing one component breaks the whole circuit; in parallel, other components still work.
Q4: Draw the circuit symbols for: a cell, a bulb, a closed switch, a resistor.
画电路符号
Cell: ─┤├─ Bulb: ─⨀─ Closed switch: ─◡─ Resistor: ─▔▔─
Q5: What is a short circuit and why is it dangerous?
短路的定义和危险
A short circuit happens when a low-resistance wire is connected across a component. It causes a very large current, which can lead to fire.
Q6: What is the function of a fuse?
保险丝的作用
A fuse melts and breaks the circuit when the current is too large, protecting the circuit from damage.
Q7: A 500 W television is used for 6 hours. The cost of electricity is $1.2 per kWh. Calculate the cost.
电费计算
Power = 500 W = 0.5 kW. Energy = 0.5 × 6 = 3 kWh. Cost = 3 × $1.2 = $3.6
Q8: What is the difference between AC (alternating current) and DC (direct current)?
交流电和直流电的区别
AC changes direction periodically; DC flows in one direction only. Household electricity uses AC; batteries provide DC.

📘 Assignment Book 综合电路题

以下题目来自 Assignment Book 2A Unit 8 Integrated Exercise。

📝 A circuit has 3 bulbs in series. One bulb is removed. What happens to the other two?
Both go out because the circuit is broken.
📝 In a parallel circuit with 2 bulbs, what happens to the brightness if one bulb is removed?
The other bulb stays at the same brightness.
📝 Why is a short circuit dangerous in household wiring?
It causes a very large current, which can overheat the wire and cause a fire.
📝 A 3 kW air conditioner runs for 4 hours. Cost = $1.5/kWh. Calculate the cost.
Energy = 3×4 = 12 kWh. Cost = 12×$1.5 = $18
📝 State the function of a fuse in a household circuit.
To melt and break the circuit when the current exceeds a safe level.
