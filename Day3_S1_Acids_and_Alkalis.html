📊 pH 标度 必考

0714
酸性中性碱性

pH < 7: 酸性 (acidic)

pH = 7: 中性 (neutral) — 纯水

pH > 7: 碱性 (alkaline)

Indicators（指示剂）

Litmus（石蕊试纸）： 酸→red, 碱→blue

pH paper（pH 试纸）： 对比比色卡读出 pH 值

⚗️ Neutralization（中和反应）必考

Acid + Alkali → Salt + Water
H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O

应用：

  • 农业：酸性土壤加 lime（石灰） 中和
  • 医药：胃酸过多吃 antacid（抗酸药）
  • 工业：酸性废水处理

🌧️ Acid Rain（酸雨）常考

成因： 化石燃料燃烧 → SO₂, NOₓ → 溶于雨水 → 形成酸雨

危害： 腐蚀建筑物、损害植物、影响水生生物

🔑 安全操作： 稀释酸时 → "酸入水，勿倒置"（Add acid to water, not water to acid）

✏️ 英文题目

Q1: What is the pH value of a neutral substance? Give an example.
pH 7. Pure water.
Q2: A solution turns blue litmus paper red. Is it acidic or alkaline?
Acidic.
Q3: What is the word equation for neutralization?
Acid + Alkali → Salt + Water
Q4: Name a common acid found in the laboratory.
Hydrochloric acid (HCl) / Sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄) / Nitric acid (HNO₃)
Q5: What causes acid rain? State ONE effect of acid rain.
Acid rain is caused by pollutants like SO₂ and NOₓ dissolving in rainwater. Effects include: damage to buildings, harm to plants, or harm to aquatic life.
