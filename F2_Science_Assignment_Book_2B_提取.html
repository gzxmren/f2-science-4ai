Unit 9: Acids and Alkalis (p.1–28)

9.1 Common Acids and Alkalis (p.1–4)

True or False 题型

  1. Lemons have a sour taste because they contain acids → T
  2. Acids have a slippery feel → F (Alkalis have slippery feel)
  3. Alkalis can be used as cleaning agents → T
  4. Acidic solutions conduct electricity → T
  5. All acidic solutions are corrosive → F (some like citric acid are not)

Multiple-choice 例题

  1. Use of acids: (1) preservatives ✓ (2) making soaps ✗ (3) removing grease ✗ → A
  2. Which is NOT an acid? → Soap (it's alkaline)
  3. 食物含 acetic acid → Vinegar
  4. Litmus in alkali → blue
  5. pH of pure water → 7
  6. Neutralization → acid + alkali → salt + water

题型

Unit 11: Force and Motion (p.51–68)

11.1 Motion and Distance-time Graphs (p.51–55)

题型 — 必考计算题

  • Average speed = distance ÷ time
  • 单位换算: km/h ↔ m/s
  • Distance-time graph 解读
  • 水平线 → at rest
  • 斜直线 → uniform motion
  • 线越陡 → speed越快

计算例题

  1. A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. Speed = 60 km/h
  2. Distance-time graph: identify uniform motion vs stationary
  3. 比较两个物体的速度（通过图的斜率）

11.2–11.3 Force and Gravity (p.56–60)

题型

  • 力的定义: push or pull, unit = newton (N)
  • 力的效果: 改变运动状态
  • 重力: W = mg (g = 9.8 N/kg)
  • Mass vs Weight 区分

计算例题

  1. 50 kg person → W = 50 × 9.8 = 490 N
  2. Object weight 100 N → mass = 100 ÷ 9.8 = 10.2 kg
  3. 弹簧秤测量重量

11.4–11.6 Friction, Action-Reaction, Space (p.61–68)

题型

  • 摩擦力方向: 与运动方向相反
  • 有益摩擦: 走路、刹车
  • 减少摩擦: lubricant, ball bearing
  • Air resistance: streamlined 设计减少阻力
  • Action-reaction pairs: 走路、游泳、火箭
  • Space flight: 反冲运动

Integrated Exercise 题型

  • Free-body diagram 力的图示
