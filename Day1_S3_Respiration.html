🧬 呼吸作用是什么？

呼吸作用是发生在所有活细胞（all living cells）中的化学反应。细胞用氧气分解食物（葡萄糖），释放能量，同时产生二氧化碳和水。

文字方程式 必考

Glucose + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + Energy
⚠️ 注意区分： respiration ≠ breathing（呼吸）
Respiration = 细胞内的化学反应（产生能量）
Breathing = 吸气和呼气（肺的机械运动）

✏️ 英文题目

Q1: What is the word equation for respiration?
Glucose + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + Energy
Q2: Give ONE difference between photosynthesis and respiration.
Photosynthesis needs light; respiration does not. / Photosynthesis produces O₂; respiration uses O₂. / Photosynthesis absorbs energy; respiration releases energy.
Q3: Does respiration take place in animals only? Explain.
No, respiration takes place in all living cells, including plants and animals.
Q4: Why do plants release CO₂ at night?
At night, there is no light, so photosynthesis stops. Only respiration continues, which releases CO₂.
