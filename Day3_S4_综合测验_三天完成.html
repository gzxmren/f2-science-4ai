✏️ 综合测验（共 10 题）

Q1 (Unit 9): What is the pH value of a neutral substance? Give an example.
中性物质的 pH 值和例子
pH 7. Pure water.
Q2 (Unit 9): What is the word equation for neutralization?
中和反应文字方程式
Acid + Alkali → Salt + Water
Q3 (Unit 10): List, in order, the parts of the eye that light passes through.
光线经过眼睛的顺序
Cornea → pupil → lens → retina → optic nerve → brain
Q4 (Unit 10): What is the function of the cochlea in the ear?
耳蜗的功能
It converts sound vibrations into nerve signals.
Q5 (Unit 10): Name the three main parts of the brain and their functions.
大脑三个主要部分的功能
Cerebrum (thinking/memory), Cerebellum (coordination/balance), Medulla (heartbeat/breathing).
Q6 (Unit 11): A train travels 360 km in 3 hours. What is its average speed?
平均速度计算
360 ÷ 3 = 120 km/h
Q7 (Unit 11): Calculate the weight of a 70 kg person on Earth. (g = 9.8 N/kg)
重量计算
W = 70 × 9.8 = 686 N
Q8 (Unit 11): What does a straight sloping line on a distance-time graph mean?
距离-时间图的倾斜直线代表什么
The object is moving at a constant speed (uniform motion).
Q9 (Unit 9+7 跨单元): How is acid rain related to air pollutants?
酸雨与空气污染物的关系
Burning fossil fuels releases SO₂ and NOₓ. These dissolve in rainwater, forming acid rain.
Q10 (Unit 11): Give ONE example of action and reaction forces.
作用力与反作用力的例子
Walking: foot pushes ground backward (action) → ground pushes foot forward (reaction). Or: rocket pushes gas downward → gas pushes rocket upward.

📘 Assignment Book 综合应用题

以下综合题融合了 Assignment Book 2A & 2B 的 Integrated Exercise 题型。

📝 Integrated: A student has a colourless gas. When she puts a glowing splint into it, the splint goes out. She then bubbles the gas through limewater and it turns milky. What is the gas?
Carbon dioxide (CO₂). Glowing splint goes out + limewater turns milky = CO₂.
📝 Integrated: A circuit has two bulbs connected in parallel and a switch in the main loop. Draw the circuit diagram.
Cell ─┤├─│Switch─◡─┴─⨀─┘ └─⨀─┘ (parallel branches with bulbs)
📝 Integrated: A student tests four solutions with litmus paper. Solution A turns litmus red. Solution B turns litmus blue. Solution C does not change the colour. Solution D also turns litmus red. Which solutions are acidic?
A and D (they turn blue litmus red).
📝 Integrated: A 0.5 kg rock is dropped on the Moon (g = 1.6 N/kg). What is its weight? Compare that to its weight on Earth (g = 9.8 N/kg).
Moon: W = 0.5 × 1.6 = 0.8 N. Earth: W = 0.5 × 9.8 = 4.9 N. Mass stays the same (0.5 kg).
