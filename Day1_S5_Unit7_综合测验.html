✏️ 综合测验（共 10 题）

孩子用英文口头或纸笔回答，写完再看答案。

Q1: Name the four most abundant gases in air and their percentages.
空气中最多的四种气体及比例
Nitrogen 78%, Oxygen 21%, Noble gases 0.9%, Carbon dioxide 0.03%
Q2: Describe how to test for oxygen gas.
怎样测试氧气？
Insert a glowing splint. If it relights, the gas is oxygen.
Q3: Describe how to test for carbon dioxide gas.
怎样测试二氧化碳？
Bubble the gas through limewater. If it turns milky/cloudy, the gas is carbon dioxide.
Q4: Write the word equation for photosynthesis.
光合作用的文字方程式
Carbon dioxide + Water → Glucose + Oxygen (need light and chlorophyll)
Q5: Write the word equation for respiration.
呼吸作用的文字方程式
Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy
Q6: Give TWO differences between photosynthesis and respiration.
光合作用和呼吸作用的两个不同点
(1) Photosynthesis needs light, respiration does not. (2) Photosynthesis absorbs energy, respiration releases energy.
Q7: Through which structure do plants exchange gases?
植物通过什么结构交换气体？
Stomata (plural of stoma) on the leaf surface.
Q8: Why do plants release carbon dioxide at night?
为什么植物夜晚释放二氧化碳？
At night, there is no light, so photosynthesis stops. Only respiration continues, which releases CO₂.
Q9: Name TWO air pollutants and state their effects on health.
两种空气污染物及其健康影响
(1) SO₂ (sulphur dioxide): causes respiratory irritation, contributes to acid rain. (2) CO (carbon monoxide): reduces the blood's ability to carry oxygen.
Q10: What is the difference between mass and weight? (Hint: this is from the appendix, but a common question!)
mass 和 weight 的区别
Mass is the amount of matter in an object (measured in kg). Weight is the force of gravity acting on an object (measured in N). Weight = mass × gravitational acceleration (W = mg).

📘 Assignment Book 真题 — Unit 7 综合题

火星大气成分分析题（Assignment Book 2A p.3）

火星大气成分： CO₂ 95.32%, N₂ 2.7%, O₂ 0.13%, CO 0.07%, H₂O vapour 0.03%, Others 1.75%
📝 (a) Why is Mars unsuitable for humans to live on?
The oxygen content is too low (only 0.13%).
📝 (b) Suggest a test to confirm the presence of CO₂ in Mars' atmosphere.
Bubble the gas through limewater. If it turns milky, CO₂ is present.
📝 (c) The amount of water vapour on Mars is very low. Describe a test for water.
Use cobalt chloride paper. It turns from blue to pink if water is present.
