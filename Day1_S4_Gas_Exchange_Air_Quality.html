🔄 气体交换（Gas Exchange）

🌱 Plants（植物）

气体交换通过叶片表面的 stomata（气孔） 进行。

Daytime（白天）：
Photosynthesis rate > Respiration rate
→ Net O₂ released（净释放氧气）
→ Net CO₂ absorbed（净吸收二氧化碳）
Night（夜晚）：
Only respiration (no light → no photosynthesis)
→ Net CO₂ released（净释放二氧化碳）

🧑 Human（人体）

气体交换通过 lungs（肺） 进行。

吸入 O₂ → 进入血液 → 输送到身体细胞用于呼吸作用

细胞产生的 CO₂ → 血液输送到肺 → 呼出体外

⚖️ 碳氧平衡 常考

自然界的 CO₂ 和 O₂ 通过两个主要过程保持平衡：

🌿 Photosynthesis
CO₂ → O₂
🧬 Respiration
O₂ → CO₂
🔥 Combustion
燃烧化石燃料 → CO₂

人为活动（燃烧化石燃料、砍伐森林）会打破这个平衡，导致 CO₂ 上升，造成 global warming（全球变暖）。

🏭 空气质量（Air Quality）

空气污染物 常考

污染物来源健康影响
Sulphur dioxide (SO₂)燃烧化石燃料呼吸道刺激，形成酸雨
Nitrogen oxides (NOₓ)汽车尾气呼吸道问题，形成酸雨
Carbon monoxide (CO)不完全燃烧降低血液携氧能力
Ozone (O₃)光化学反应刺激眼睛和呼吸道
Particulates（悬浮粒子）燃烧、工业肺部疾病
AQHI（空气质量健康指数）：
级别越高，健康风险越大。应减少户外活动。

✏️ 英文题目

Q1: Through which structure do plants exchange gases?
Stomata (on the leaf surface)
Q2: What happens to the net gas exchange in plants at night? Why?
At night, only respiration takes place (no light → no photosynthesis). Net CO₂ is released.
Q3: Name TWO human activities that increase the CO₂ level in the atmosphere.
Burning fossil fuels / Deforestation
Q4: Name ONE air pollutant and state its effect on health.
SO₂: causes respiratory irritation / CO: reduces blood's ability to carry oxygen
Q5: What is acid rain?
Rain that contains acids formed when pollutants like SO₂ and NOₓ dissolve in rainwater.

📝 Through which structure do plants exchange gases?
Stomata (on the leaf surface)
📝 What colour does hydrogencarbonate indicator turn with more CO₂? With less CO₂?
More CO₂ → yellow. Less CO₂ → purple. (Normal air → red)
📝 Name TWO human activities that increase CO₂ in the atmosphere.
Burning fossil fuels and deforestation.
📝 Name ONE air pollutant and state its effect.
SO₂: causes respiratory irritation / CO: reduces oxygen in blood
📝 What is the AQHI used for?
It indicates the health risk from air pollution. Higher levels = higher risk.
