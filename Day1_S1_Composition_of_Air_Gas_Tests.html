🧪 空气的成分 常考

空气是 mixture（混合物），不是 compound（化合物）。

Nitrogen (N₂)
78%
Oxygen (O₂)
21%
Noble gases
0.9%
CO₂
0.03%
Water vapour + others
0.07%
⚠️ 考试陷阱： CO₂ 只有 0.03%，不是 3% 或 0.3%。

🔥 气体测试 必考实验

翻到课本 Experiment 7.1，看对应的实验图。

🔵 Oxygen（氧气）

Burning splint → burns more brightly
Glowing splint → relights ✅

🔴 Carbon dioxide（二氧化碳）

Burning splint → goes out
Glowing splint → goes out
Limewater → turns milky/cloudy
📌 考试必背句：
"Insert a glowing splint into the gas. If it relights, the gas is oxygen."
"Bubble the gas through limewater. If it turns milky, the gas is carbon dioxide."

✏️ 英文题目（口头回答）

Q1: Which gas makes up 78% of air?
Nitrogen (N₂)
Q2: What is the percentage of oxygen in air?
21%
Q3: A gas turns limewater milky. What is the gas?
Carbon dioxide (CO₂)
Q4: How would you test for oxygen gas?
Insert a glowing splint. If it relights, the gas is oxygen.
Q5: What happens to a burning splint when it is put into carbon dioxide?
It goes out.

📘 Assignment Book 真题 — 7.1 Air

以下题目来自 Assignment Book 2A。

📝 True or False: Nitrogen is a reactive gas.
False. Nitrogen is very unreactive.
📝 True or False: Oxygen can relight a burning splint.
False. (Glowing splint, not burning splint.)
📝 True or False: Noble gases do not have any daily applications.
False. (Used in balloons, lighting, etc.)
📝 MC: Which gases can be found in air? (1) Argon (2) Water vapour (3) CO₂
(1), (2) and (3) — all three
📝 MC: Mars air: CO₂ 95.32%, O₂ 0.13%. Why unsuitable for humans?
The oxygen content is too low (0.13%).
📝 MC: Gas X CANNOT relight a glowing splint. X can be? (1) CO₂ (2) O₂ (3) N₂
(1) and (3) only — CO₂ and N₂
