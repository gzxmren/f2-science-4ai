📊 三大概念对比

⚡ Current（电流）
单位： ampere (A)
测量： ammeter → 串联 in series
本质： 自由电子定向移动
开路→电子随机运动 | 闭路→形成 net flow
⚡ Voltage（电压）
单位： volt (V)
测量： voltmeter → 并联 in parallel
本质： 推动电流的"压力"
电池串联 → 总电压增加
⚡ Resistance（电阻）
单位： ohm (Ω)
本质： 阻碍电流流动
电阻越大 → 电流越小
导体电阻小 | 绝缘体电阻极大
🔑 记忆口诀：
安培计测电流 → 串 联（A 在电路中）
伏特计测电压 → 并 联（V 在元件两端）

✏️ 英文题目

Q1: What is the unit of electric current?
Ampere (A)
Q2: How should an ammeter be connected in a circuit?
In series with the component.
Q3: How should a voltmeter be connected in a circuit?
In parallel with the component.
Q4: What happens to the total voltage when more cells are connected in series?
The total voltage increases.
Q5: What is resistance and what is its unit?
Resistance is a measure of how much a component opposes the flow of current. Its unit is the ohm (Ω).

📘 Assignment Book 真题 — 8.3–8.5 Current, Voltage, Resistance

以下题目来自 Assignment Book 2A。

📝 True or False: An ammeter should be connected in parallel in a circuit.
False. An ammeter should be connected in series.
📝 True or False: The unit of voltage is the ampere.
False. Voltage is measured in volts (V). Ampere is the unit of current.
📝 MC: What happens when more cells are connected in series?
The total voltage increases.
📝 MC: Which component is used to measure electric current?
An ammeter (connected in series).

Ohm (Ω).
